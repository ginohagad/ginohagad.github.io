Three Steps, One Way

The path to salvation is simple yet profound

A

ACKNOWLEDGE

Acknowledge that you are a sinner. And only by the blood of Jesus that your sins can be forgiven. With His blood my sins are cleansed and will cleanse you from all unrighteousness.

💔
B

BELIEVE

Believe in the Lord Jesus. That he was sent to die on the cross for the forgiveness of our sins.

🙏
C

CONFESS

Confess with your mouth that Jesus is your Lord and believe in your heart that He was raised from the dead.

🗣️
🎁

Receiving Jesus as your Lord and Savior brings The Gift of Eternal Life

"For by grace we are saved through faith not by works so that no man will boast."

Ephesians 2:8-9

🙏

Acceptance Prayer

Dear Heavenly Father I confess that I am a sinner and acknowledge that I cannot save myself. Thank you for sending your Son Jesus to die on the cross for the forgiveness of my sins. I am washed and I am forgiven.

Now, I confess with my mouth that Jesus is my Lord and my Savior. And believe in my heart that Jesus was raised from the dead.

With this I am saved and I received the gift of eternal life. I believe my name has now been written in the book of life and become your child. This I pray in Jesus Name,

Amen.
